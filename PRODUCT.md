# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

KDN (Kementerian Dalam Negeri) staff. Hub entry point to two internal tools.

## Product Purpose

Landing index for KDN iLPF — routes users to Moving Forward (performance tracking) or Profil (profile analytics dashboard).

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, precise, calm. Dark. Government-grade trust without bureaucratic heaviness.

## Anti-references

Bright SaaS landing pages. Generic gov portal designs (white backgrounds, blue banners). Identical card grids.

## Design Principles

1. Directory, not a billboard — it routes, doesn't sell
2. Typography carries the weight — Fraunces + Inter, no decoration needed
3. Each entry feels distinct through accent color, not structure

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Malay-primary language.
