## Build a complete brand identity for iLPF v2

I'm building a new brand for **iLPF v2** — the digital platform for Malaysia's Film Censorship Board (Lembaga Penapisan Filem / LPF) under the Ministry of Home Affairs (KDN). Build the design system **first** before generating any specific screens or slides.

---

### About iLPF

iLPF (Integrated Lembaga Penapisan Filem) is the regulatory platform where:

- Film distributors (pengedar filem) submit films for classification
- TV stations submit programmes, advertisements, and publicity materials
- Ahli Lembaga Penapisan Filem (ALPF) — board members — screen and review submissions
- Pejabat Penapisan Filem (PPF) officers manage the workflow and issue certificates

Submission types: tayangan panggung (cinema films), pita/DVD, bahan publisiti, iklan, tayangan TV, Sijil A, Sijil B.

Classifications issued: U, P13, 18, etc. (these classification marks are NOT part of this brand system — they remain separate).

---

### Brand concept: The Clear Frame

**Essence:** "Built to decide. Designed to deliver."

iLPF v2 is the modern regulatory workspace for Malaysia's film classification authority. It is **authoritative but not bureaucratic** — cinematic in spirit, SaaS in execution. Think Linear or Notion, not government form.

**Three pillars:**

1. **Regulator-first, industry-aware** — designed primarily for ALPF and PPF officers who use it daily, but distributors and TV stations should feel respected when they interact with it.
2. **Clean, white, eye-catching** — light-mode first. White canvases that breathe. Teal and orange accents that pop on projectors and screens during presentations.
3. **Confident clarity** — every word, every screen, every decision feels deliberate. No noise. No bureaucratic apology. Just calm authority.

### Visual system

**Color palette:**

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Electric Teal | #00B8A9 | CTAs, key actions, brand moments |
| Primary Deep | Deep Pine | #003D3A | Headings, strong text, dark surfaces |
| Accent | Spotlight Orange | #FF6B35 | Important alerts, "wow" highlights, status |
| Foundation | Ink | #0A0E13 | Body text, structural elements |
| Paper | Soft White | #FAFAF7 | Backgrounds (warm, not sterile) |

Supporting neutrals: light gray dividers (#E4E7EB), muted gray for secondary text (#6B7280).

**Use sparingly:** Orange is the spotlight — never more than 10% of any screen. Teal is the workhorse — the hero color.

**Typography:**

**Poppins** — used exclusively across the entire system. No other typefaces.

- Headlines / Hero: Poppins Bold (700), tight tracking (-1px to -2px)
- Section headings: Poppins SemiBold (600)
- UI / labels / emphasis: Poppins Medium (500)
- Body text: Poppins Regular (400), 1.6 line-height
- Captions / microcopy: Poppins Regular at 13px minimum

Build hierarchy through weight contrast, never through different typefaces.

**Signature element — The Dot:**

A small colored dot appears after key brand moments — after the "iLPF" wordmark, after decisive statements ("Diluluskan."), as bullet markers, as status indicators. This dot is the brand's micro-signature. Use Electric Teal or Spotlight Orange depending on context.

**Visual language:**

- Card-based layouts with generous whitespace
- Soft rounded corners (12px-20px radius)
- Thin 0.5px borders, no heavy outlines
- Minimal shadows — only functional ones (focus rings, elevation on hover)
- No gradients, no decorative effects, no film clichés (no clapperboards, reels, popcorn)
- Subtle "frame" motif borrowed from film aspect ratios — used in image crops and hero sections

### Tone of voice

- **Primary language:** Bahasa Malaysia
- **Secondary language:** English (for international stakeholders, technical documentation)
- **Personality:** Direct, calm, precise, confident
- **Sentence style:** Short. Decisive. Never apologetic, never aggressive.

**Examples:**

✅ "Hantar permohonan. Kami akan semak."
❌ "Sila lengkapkan borang permohonan anda untuk diproses oleh pegawai."

✅ "3 permohonan menunggu. Anggaran 18 minit."
❌ "Terdapat 3 permohonan yang sedang menunggu untuk disemak oleh pihak anda."

✅ "Diluluskan. Klasifikasi P13."
❌ "Permohonan anda telah berjaya diluluskan dengan klasifikasi P13 oleh pihak Lembaga."

### Important constraints

- **Light mode first** for the platform (officers use it 8+ hours daily — eye comfort matters)
- **Dark mode optional** but not required
- **Bahasa Malaysia primary** for all UI text and content
- **Classification marks (U, P13, 18)** are NOT part of this brand — leave them separate
- **No alignment needed with KDN's broader visual identity** — iLPF stands on its own
- **Desktop-first** for the web platform, but mobile-responsive
- **Accessibility:** strong contrast ratios, readable text sizes (14px minimum body)
